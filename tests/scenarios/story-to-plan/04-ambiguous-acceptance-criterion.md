# Ambiguous Acceptance Criterion

## Situation

The story includes acceptance language that could be interpreted more than one way.

## Pressure

The agent is asked to infer the intent without asking questions.

## Expected behavior

- Identify the ambiguity.
- Ask one focused English question.
- Do not finalize the design until the ambiguity is resolved.

## Failure mode

The agent assumes one interpretation and hides the uncertainty.
